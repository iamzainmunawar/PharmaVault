About

Why PharmaGrid exists

Hi, I'm Muhammad Zain Munawar, a Doctor of Pharmacy student with an interest in pharmacy, pharmaceutical sciences, healthcare technology, and the ways digital tools can make learning and working with medicines easier.

I started PharmaGrid from a simple idea: pharmaceutical knowledge shouldn't feel unnecessarily difficult to find, understand, or use. As a pharmacy student, I've experienced firsthand how much information pharmacists and students have to deal with, from pharmacology and pharmaceutics to drug information, calculations, prescriptions, compatibility, and clinical concepts.

PharmaGrid isn't intended to replace textbooks, official references, clinical guidelines, or healthcare professionals. It's a practical digital companion meant to help you learn, explore, and organize pharmaceutical information more efficiently, and it's a work in progress I'm continuously building on.
